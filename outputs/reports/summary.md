## Assumptions & Limitations
- Sentinel-2: S2_SR_HARMONIZED (surface reflectance) preferred; S2_TOA_HARMONIZED only for months with no usable SR scenes (see tier counts above). Scene filter: <60% scene cloud, falling back to <80%, plus pixel-level Cloud Score+ (cs_cdf ≥ 0.6) where available.
- SSMI: primary source is NASA SMAP SPL4SMGP (10 km soil moisture, 2015+) standardised per tehsil per calendar month; months without SMAP fall back to a precipitation − ET₀ water-deficit SSMI (flagged in ssmi_source column).
- CDI weights: SPI .35, SSMI .25, VCI(z) .25, EVI .10, PCI .05; thresholds extreme ≤ −2.0, severe ≤ −1.5, moderate ≤ −1.0, mild ≤ 0.0.
- CDI is computed per pixel (100 m) over the district and averaged per tehsil; per-tehsil zonal statistics are exact means over district-intersecting pixels.
- Random Forest: two labels (composite CDI stage and SPI-3-only stage); features are SPI/SSMI/NDVI/EVI/PCI only — CDI is never fed back as a feature.
- Weather API/archive (Open-Meteo) provides the precipitation, ET₀ and temperature series; no station data is used.
